EDITOR RESIZE
- checkSizeConstraint() reads only width (gbEditor.mm:198-215) so a bottom-edge drag has its height discarded and recomputed from the unchanged width, and the window fights the drag vertically - fit the aspect box to whichever dimension the host actually changed
- Content repaints only on the 30 Hz timer, so during a live resize it lags the window edge - G2-Edit redraws on demand and stays in step; override -setFrameSize: in gbView.m to redraw at once
- Two masters on the frame: attached() sets NSViewWidthSizable|NSViewHeightSizable (gbEditor.mm:107) AND onSize() setFrame:s, in no guaranteed order - drop the autoresizing mask and let onSize() be the only authority
- Minor: the (int32) truncations in checkSizeConstraint let a repeated resize creep a pixel at a time
- Minor: plugFrame is stored but never used - the missing half if the plug-in is ever to REQUEST a size rather than accept one
